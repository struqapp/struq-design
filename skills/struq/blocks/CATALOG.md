A plain editorial section (statement, paragraph, one photo, one text link) that enters on a short scroll-scrubbed stretch in one of three ways: lines rising through masks with the photo opening from its bottom edge, the photo opening from a thin slit with the type rising beside it, or the whole section laid over the previous one like a card, which dims and falls behind; scrolling back runs every entrance backwards.

Variants (`variant`, the first is the default):
- `lines`: The statement's lines rise through masks one after another, the paragraph and link follow softly, then the photo opens from its bottom edge.
- `clip`: The photo leads, opening from a thin horizontal slit inside its final frame to full size; the type rises beside it once the slit is half open.
- `card`: The whole section slides up over the previous one with a hairline shadow on its top edge; the covered section dims and falls behind as the content settles.

### footer-reveal
film: role frame · home none · motif neutral · energy 2 · pins 0 · none

Footer: revealed from under the page. The page's last section lifts away like a sheet and uncovers the footer already waiting beneath it (the name large at a light weight, a closing line, the contact address, a few plain links), scrubbed to the scroll and laid down again on the way back.

Variants (`variant`, the first is the default):
- `lift`: The last section rises off the footer, which stands still beneath it; its leaving edge carries a hairline and a short soft shadow.
- `fold`: The last section tilts back a few degrees as it lifts, like a page turned up, and the footer brightens from under a shade as it is uncovered.
- `iris`: The footer is uncovered through an opening that grows from a thin slit at the bottom of the screen to full, the name rising inside it.
