# Code of conduct

Struq is a small project and wants to stay a good place to ask a question or show your work.

- Be kind and assume good intent. People here are at every level, and plenty of them are new to coding agents.
- Critique the work, never the person. "The hero loses its contrast on phones" helps; anything about the author does
  not.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexual content or personal attacks, in issues, discussions, pull requests or
  anywhere the project lives.
- Do not share other people's private information, or a client's site they have not agreed to show.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove anything that breaks these rules, and block people who keep breaking them.

To report something, write to [support@struq.nl](mailto:support@struq.nl). Reports are read in confidence.
